# Chonk

A slightly chubby British Shorthair who lives in your MacBook's notch and feeds on your focus.
Focus and he grows. Get distracted and he leaves. Pet him, feed him, play with him.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/ozzider/chonk/releases/latest)** (macOS 15 or later)

## Install

1. Download `Chonk-<version>.dmg` from the latest release and open it.
2. Drag Chonk into Applications and open it.
3. macOS says it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to
   **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Chonk.

After that Chonk updates itself: it checks once a day and installs new versions in the background.

---

**Nederlands:** download de nieuwste versie via de link hierboven, sleep Chonk naar Programma's en kies bij de eerste keer
**Systeeminstellingen › Privacy en beveiliging › Toch openen**. Daarna werkt Chonk zichzelf automatisch bij.

This repository only hosts downloads and update feeds.
